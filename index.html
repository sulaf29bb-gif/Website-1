Developing an automated calibration rig for precise, reliable sensor calibration at Kongsberg Automotive.

RIG-444

The R‑444 Common Calibration Rig project is a bachelor's project that focuses on developing a Mostly Automated and versatile calibration system for Kongsberg Automotive in‑house laboratory.

The rig is designed to streamline the calibration of a wide range of sensors by reducing manual work, eliminating variation, and ensuring repeatable accuracy across all testing- environments.

  • Various Sensor Calibration
  • Testing on Functional Rigs
  • Advanced Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Kongsberg Automative

KA is a global technology supplier with a strong emphasis on innovation, engineering quality, and robust research and development practices.

The company invests heavily in R&D and operates a network of global technology.
The KA laboratory in Kongsberg plays a central role in ensuring product quality through rigorous data acquisition, calibration, and sensor validation

Project Goals

What Are We Trying To Achieve?

Automation: 

Automating most of the primary calibration types of sensors to reduce time and uncertainty.

Stability:

Create a robust system that withstands long periods of use.

Repeatability:

Provide an easy System that can be utilized by several Personnel, with minimal Chance of human errors.

UNIFICATION:

Providing a unified tool for calibrating multitple sensor types.

Documentation:

Delivering fully comrehensive report, potential training for KA personnel.
